## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite interactive data journalism experience covering the Ho Chi Minh City Master Plan (2025–2050), drawing direct influence from modern digital investigative platforms like *The New York Times Graphics*, *Reuters Graphics*, and *The Pudding*. 

The design combines **Modern Editorial** with **Data-Driven Precision**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, visionary, analytical, yet visually inviting and accessible.
- **Narrative Vehicle:** Scrollytelling visual essays, layered cartographic visualisations, sticky timeline panels, and oversized metric highlights.
- **Visual Texture:** Crisp micro-grids, delicate framing rules, luminous mesh-gradient backdrops, and high-legibility sans-serif typography. Avoid heavy skeuomorphism and muddy drop shadows; prioritize fluid interaction, clean data ink ratios, and luminous spatial clarity.

## Colors

The palette operates in a crisp, bright color mode built to support multi-layered thematic urban cartography and data hierarchies.

- **Canvas & Backdrops:**
  - Base Canvas: Porcelain White (`#F8FAFC`).
  - Elevated Container / Paper Surface: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Subtle Mesh Underlays: Linear and radial blurs transitioning softly between Sky Mist (`#E0F2FE`) and Mint Dew (`#F0FDF4`).
- **Primary Data Tones:**
  - **Ocean Blue (`#0284C7`):** Primary narrative anchors, water infrastructure, central urban corridors, and active interactive controls.
  - **Coral Orange (`#F97316`):** Secondary accent, population density spikes, industrial/logistics transformations, and primary callouts.
  - **Emerald Green (`#10B981`):** Tertiary accent, ecological buffers, parks, green infrastructure, and sustainable transit networks.
- **Extended Categorical Palette (Urban Planning Themes):**
  - **Electric Violet (`#7C3AED`):** High-tech zones, Innovation Districts (Thu Duc City), and digital infrastructure.
  - **Amber Gold (`#EAB308`):** Heritage conservation, mixed-use transit-oriented developments (TOD), and airport/seaport hubs.
- **Neutrals & Borders:**
  - High-Contrast Ink: Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) for headlines and critical metrics.
  - Body Text: Charcoal (`#334155`).
  - Subtle Metadata: Muted Slate (`#64748B`).
  - Architectural Hairlines: Clean Slate Border (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a sharp editorial tone balancing high-impact structural headlines with effortless long-form reading and precise numeric legibility.

- **Headlines & Big Stats (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a clean, modern, slightly architectural geometric punch. Reserved for scrollytelling milestones, section leads, and key percentage/population statistics.
- **Narrative Text (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Offers high x-height, neutral neutrality, and comfortable tracking across dense analytical paragraphs.
- **Data Points & Annotations (`JetBrains Mono`):** Delivers cartographic coordinates, census year badges, interactive sliders, legends, and chart axis markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts the rhythm of prestige editorial newsrooms for rich interactive screens.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop uses a 12-column fluid grid system with a maximum reading canvas of `1440px`.
  - Scrollytelling side-by-side mode: 5 columns dedicated to sticky narrative steps (`40%` width) and 7 columns assigned to the interactive stage/map canvas (`60%` width).
  - Editorial full-bleed mode: Maps and charts occupy all 12 columns, with annotations anchored via fluid overlays.
- **Scrollytelling Step Pacing:**
  - Each narrative scroll trigger card is spaced with `space-3xl` top/bottom margins to allow full visual transitions of data layers before the next milestone activates.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked mode. Sticky map pinned to the top 45vh; story cards slide over bottom canvas with 1rem margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, compact margins (`2rem`), charts scale proportionally.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column dual-axis scrollytelling stage.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layers and crisp low-contrast hairlines** rather than muddy physical shadows, ensuring maps and vector data remain the focal point.

- **Base Level (Canvas):** Porcelain White (`#F8FAFC`) with soft, atmospheric radial mesh gradients (`#E0F2FE` and `#F0FDF4` at 40%–60% opacity) suggesting topography and waterways.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Step Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by precise hairlines (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Layer 2 (Floating Scrollytelling Panels & Tooltips):** Semi-opaque frosted backdrops (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`).
- **Shadow System:** Ambient, low-intensity elevation only on floating inspector cards: `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design uses **Level 1 (Soft)** shape geometry to retain an exacting architectural and cartographic feel:

- **Base Corners:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) for buttons, badges, tooltip flags, and input selectors.
- **Medium Structural Elements:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) for floating scrollytelling step containers, chart panels, and modal inspectors.
- **Large Panels:** `12px` (`0.75rem`) for viewport-filling data modules and docked interactive tool trays.
- **Data Pills & Status Dots:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) reserved strictly for year toggles (e.g., `2025`, `2035`, `2050`), live filters, and legend keys.

## Components

### 1. Scrollytelling Narrative Step Cards
- **Structure:** Floating white card (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` blur filter) framed in `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Content Flow:** Mono section index (e.g., `PHASE 02 // 2030–2035`) in `JetBrains Mono` using Ocean Blue (`#0284C7`), followed by a `Space Grotesk` subhead and concise editorial body text.
- **State Changes:** Non-active steps fade to `0.35` opacity; active in-view steps transition to `1.0` opacity with an animated left accent border (3px solid `#0284C7`).

### 2. Big Stat Metric Blocks (Data Callouts)
- **Visuals:** Massive numeral in `Space Grotesk` (`stat-display`) with an inline Delta badge (e.g., `+42%`).
- **Color Coding:** Direct semantic tie: Green (`#10B981`) for green coverage/public transport capacity; Orange (`#F97316`) for population shift/economic output; Violet (`#7C3AED`) for tech employment.
- **Label:** Micro-label placed underneath in `data-mono-sm` uppercase.

### 3. Interactive Year Slider & Milestones (2025–2050)
- **Control Bar:** Floating glass dock at the bottom center of the viewport.
- **Thumb/Nodes:** Discrete step markers for `2025`, `2030`, `2035`, `2040`, `2050`.
- **Active State:** Highlighted pill with `#0284C7` fill and white label; connecting track fills dynamically using a smooth Ocean Blue to Coral Orange gradient.

### 4. Interactive Map Layer Toggles & Chips
- **Resting:** Porcelain White container, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, slate text with a muted circular vector indicator.
- **Selected:** Border tinted to match layer topic (e.g., `#10B981` for "Vành đai sinh thái"), light background tint (e.g., `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`), and solid circular swatch.

### 5. Cartographic Micro-Legends & Hover Tooltips
- **Tooltip:** Strict monospace coordinates and numeric value pairs (`Mật độ: 14.200 người/km²`).
- **Surface:** Pure `#FFFFFF` card with deep slate micro-typography and direct thematic color bar on the left edge.

### 6. Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Khám phá quy hoạch 3D"):** Solid `#0284C7` background, white label, sharp micro-interaction (`transform: translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary / Methodology Trigger:** Transparent with border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`, hover fill `#F1F5F9`.
